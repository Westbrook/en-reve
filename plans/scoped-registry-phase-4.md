## Verified results

With focus followed by the prescribed 500 ms wait, throttled first-command readiness improves from 259.85 to 73.00 ms (−71.91%). The paired difference is −186.85 ms, with an exploratory 95% interval of −190.95 to −183.15 ms. Input focus improves from 236.15 to 48.75 ms. The deliberate wait is excluded from action latency; it is an available preparation opportunity, not a prediction-accuracy or total-session-time result.

Immediate use improves modestly: throttled readiness 259.60 → 250.70 ms (−3.43%; difference interval −15.90 to −1.25 ms), input focus 236.60 → 227.55 ms. There is no deliberate advance wait, but normal trigger focus can start preparation just before Enter. A missed preparation opportunity still leaves substantial first-use latency.

Desktop first-use medians, immediate / advance-intent: Chromium 59.05 → 47.90 ms / 62.15 → 27.15 ms; Firefox 42.50 → 30.00 ms / 34.50 → 18.00 ms; WebKit 45.00 → 44.00 ms / 44.50 → 40.50 ms. Chromium and Firefox have negative paired intervals for both conditions. WebKit intervals include or touch zero, so its smaller changes do not establish a clear improvement.

The intent policy adds 481 gzip JavaScript bytes at startup (+0.13% in Chromium) and 31 gzip HTML bytes. The optional command code contributes 3,823 additional gzip bytes before an advance-intent action in Chromium, even if that action is abandoned. Request counts remain 180 at startup and 182 after use. Definitions stay unregistered until explicit opening. Every startup-readiness interval includes zero; no startup-speed improvement or equivalence is claimed.

Production retention has the same 3,131 nodes and three documents at cycle 100, with 253 versus 251 listeners: two bounded intent handlers. Neither arm shows node/listener/document growth from cycles 10 to 100. Median heap growth is 734,180 → 732,980 bytes; retained heap at cycle 100 differs by +2,336 bytes. Five repetitions per arm are descriptive, not proof of leak-free behavior.

The separate Chromium mechanism study confirms selected activation: with four boundaries of 250 hosts, only the chosen 250 upgrade. Dedicated and shared-null native policies initially connect all 1,000 undefined hosts; the global-template policy initially connects none and materializes only the requested boundary. Inert templates still allocate DOM nodes: zero connected hosts is not zero total DOM or a demonstrated heap saving. The minimal-constructor timing study is not a production-component speedup comparison.

Across 15 controller disposal runs, node, listener and document growth from cycles 10 to 100 is zero for every policy. Median heap growth is 7,916 bytes for dedicated registries, 9,296 for shared-null islands and 4,724 for template fallback. Controllers are disposed and application-owned roots removed; this differs from production retention, which keeps one workflow alive.
